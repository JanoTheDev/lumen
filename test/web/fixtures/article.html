Leaders agree climate deal

Leaders from 120 countries agreed a climate deal at the summit in Nairobi on Tuesday, after two weeks of talks.

The deal commits rich countries to cut emissions by half by 2035 and to fund clean energy in poorer nations.

Critics said the targets are not binding and that the money promised falls short of what is needed.

The agreement will be signed formally next month in Geneva, officials said.
