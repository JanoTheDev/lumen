# Outlook (classic) glossary

- **Ribbon** — the band of tabs and large buttons across the top of the window.
- **Reading pane** — the area that shows the selected email next to the list.
- **Message window** — a separate window for writing or reading one email.
- **Inline reply** — a reply written inside the reading pane instead of its own window.
- **Pop Out** — moves an inline reply into its own message window.
- **Check Names** — matches typed names against the address book and underlines the match.
- **Deleted Items** — where deleted email goes; it can be moved back from there.
- **Archive** — a folder for email you want to keep but not see in the Inbox.
- **Quick Steps** — saved multi-step actions on the Home tab, such as Move to a folder.
- **Focused Inbox** — splits the Inbox into Focused and Other tabs.
