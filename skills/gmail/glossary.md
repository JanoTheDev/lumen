# Gmail glossary

- **Conversation** — an email and all its replies, shown as one row in the list.
- **Archive** — takes an email out of the Inbox without deleting it; it stays in All Mail and in search.
- **Label** — Gmail's version of a folder; one email can have several.
- **Draft** — an email that is written but not sent; Gmail saves it in Drafts as you type.
- **Compose window** — the box where a new email, reply or forward is written.
- **Recipient chip** — the rounded name bubble a recipient becomes in the To field once picked.
- **Inbox tabs** — Primary, Promotions, Social and Updates, when the user has them on.
- **Snooze** — hides an email until a chosen time, then brings it back to the top of the Inbox.
- **Trash** — deleted email waits here for 30 days before it is gone.
- **Spam** — junk email; reporting spam moves it there and trains the filter.
